Historical SirMagick concept notes (see README.md for current behavior)

Run a CPU-based Discord image bot in Docker.

@sirmagick [command]             Use the most recent channel image.
@sirmagick [command] [imageurl]  Process an explicitly supplied image.

Initial effect ideas:
fatmagic: detect the face and body and make them slightly blocky, similar to Minecraft.
          The current implementation widens the face and detected upper body instead.
chudthor: enlarge and square the face while shrinking its features toward the center.
warpface: enlarge the head as a connected region and shrink individual features locally.
blockface: uniformly shrink the inner face, stretching only the surrounding skin strip;
           preserve the outer contour and omit head enlargement.
deepfry: exaggerated colors, compression artifacts, and shitpost stickers.

These notes preserve the original ideas; they are not the active specification.
